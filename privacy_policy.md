
# Privacy Policy for Locked In

**Last Updated:** December 31, 2025

Thank you for using Locked In. This Privacy Policy is intended to help you understand what data we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

## Information We Collect

Locked In is a browser extension designed to help you focus by blocking websites. To provide this functionality, the extension requires access to certain browser permissions and may store data locally on your device.

We **do not** collect any personally identifiable information (PII) or any other data that could be used to identify you. All data related to the extension's functionality is stored locally on your computer using the browser's storage.

## How We Use Your Data

The data we store is used solely for the functionality of the extension. This includes:

*   **Blocked Websites List:** The list of websites you have chosen to block is stored locally.
*   **Timer Settings:** Any timer or scheduling configurations are stored locally.
*   **Usage Statistics:** To provide you with insights into your browsing habits (as shown on the dashboard), the extension may store data about the time you spend on websites. This data is also stored locally and is not transmitted to any external servers.

## Permissions

To provide its functionality, Locked In requires the following permissions:

*   **storage:** This permission is used to save your settings, such as the list of blocked websites and timer configurations, locally on your browser.
*   **webNavigation:** This permission allows the extension to monitor website navigation events to determine if a website should be blocked based on your settings.
*   **tabs:** This permission is used to interact with browser tabs, such as closing a tab or redirecting to a blocked page.
*   **scripting:** This permission is used to inject scripts into web pages to block access to websites.
*   **host_permissions (<all_urls>):** This permission is required to enable the extension to block websites on any domain you choose.

## Data Security

All data used by the Locked In extension is stored locally on your device using the browser's built-in storage mechanisms. This data is not transmitted to any external servers, and we have no access to it.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
